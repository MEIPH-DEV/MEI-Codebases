1. Product Formulary & Clinical Reference

Brand Name Active Ingredient Clinical Indications Mechanism of Action Dosing & Clinical Pearls
Mamazol Miconazole Candidal balanitis, cutaneous fungal infections, intertrigo, dermatophytosis. Inhibits ergosterol synthesis, disrupting fungal cell membrane permeability. Apply thin layer to affected area BID for 2-4 weeks. Maintain skin hygiene.
Pubergen Human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG) Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, male infertility, cryptorchidism. LH analogue; stimulates Leydig cells to produce endogenous testosterone. 1500–5000 IU IM/SC 2–3 times weekly. Preserves intratesticular T during TRT.
Gynogen Estradiol Estrogen deficiency, endocrine therapy, hormone replacement protocols. Binds nuclear estrogen receptors; regulates target gene expression and gonadotropin release. Individualized dosing based on serum levels. Monitor cardiovascular & hepatic markers.
Endogen Dienogest Progestogenic management, pelvic pain suppression, hormone-dependent tissue control. Selective progestin; suppresses ovarian estradiol secretion and induces target tissue atrophy. 2 mg daily continuously. Take at same time daily to avoid breakthrough bleeding.
Evaban Apixaban DVT/PE treatment/prophylaxis, post-surgical thromboprophylaxis. Direct, reversible FXa inhibitor; blocks thrombin generation and fibrin clot formation. 2.5 mg to 5 mg BID depending on indication and renal function.
Endmet 11.25 Leuprorelin Acetate (11.25mg) Advanced/metastatic prostate cancer (3-month extended depot ADT). Continuous GnRH receptor stimulation leading to receptor desensitization and castrate testosterone levels. 11.25 mg SC/IM once every 12 weeks. Monitor serum PSA and testosterone.
Endmet 3.75 Leuprorelin Acetate (3.75mg) Advanced prostate cancer (1-month depot ADT), hormone-sensitive tumors. GnRH receptor agonist suppressing LH/FSH secretion and androgen synthesis. 3.75 mg SC/IM monthly. Co-administer anti-androgen initially to prevent flare.
RM Clinical Guidance: Androgen Deprivation Therapy (ADT) Flare Prevention
Initiating GnRH agonists such as Endmet 3.75 or Endmet 11.25 causes an initial transient surge in serum testosterone. To prevent symptomatic disease flare (e.g., bone pain, acute urinary obstruction), co-administer an anti-androgen starting prior to or concurrently with the first depot injection.
