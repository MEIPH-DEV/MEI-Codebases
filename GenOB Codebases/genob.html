Dydrogest Dydrogesterone Threatened miscarriage, recurrent loss, luteal phase support. Orally active progestogen with selective progesterone receptor affinity. 10-40 mg daily. Supports gestation without androgenic activity.
Evamin Multivitamins Maternal nutritional maintenance, general pregnancy support. Provides vitamins necessary for maternal metabolic demands. 1 tablet daily post-meal. Prevents micronutrient depletion.
Medeprim Co-trimoxazole Susceptible urinary tract & respiratory tract infections. Blocks sequential steps in bacterial folic acid synthesis. 1 tablet DS BID. Avoid near term due to potential neonatal hyperbilirubinemia.
Mum-2-B Plus Multivitamins & Minerals Prenatal supplementation, neural tube defect prevention. Essential vitamins, minerals, and folic acid for embryogenesis. 1 capsule daily. Crucial during early organogenesis.

Tranamic Tranexamic Acid Postpartum hemorrhage (PPH), severe bleeding control. Antifibrinolytic; inhibits plasminogen activation to prevent fibrinolysis. 1 g IV over 10 mins within 3 hrs of PPH onset. Repeat in 30 mins if needed.
Erytose Erythromycin PPROM latency therapy, gestational Chlamydia infection. Macrolide; reversibly binds 50S ribosome to halt protein synthesis. 250-500 mg QID. Core component of PPROM latency regimes.
Evafer Iron Supplement Iron deficiency anemia, fetal red cell support, PPH recovery. Supplies elemental iron essential for functional hemoglobin synthesis. 60-120 mg elemental iron daily on empty stomach with Vitamin C.
Evacarb 125 Carbamazepine (125mg) Gestational seizure disorders, neuralgic pain control. Blockade of voltage-gated sodium channels in hyperexcitable neurons. 125 mg BID titrated. Supplement with high-dose Folic Acid (5mg/day).
Evacarb 250 Carbamazepine (250mg) Generalized tonic-clonic seizures, epilepsy in pregnancy. Suppresses sustained repetitive firing via sodium channel inactivation. 250 mg BID/TID. Administer Vitamin K to mother prior to delivery.
Contractocin Oxytocin Labor induction/augmentation, PPH prophylaxis & management. Stimulates uterine oxytocin receptors, increasing calcium-mediated contraction. Induction: 1-2 mU/min IV. PPH: 10 units IM or IV infusion post-delivery.
Obstetric High-Alert Protocol: Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH) Management
When addressing obstetric hemorrhage, early intervention is critical: co-administer Tranamic (Tranexamic Acid 1 g IV) alongside uterotonic agents like Contractocin (Oxytocin) within 3 hours of birth. Delay beyond 3 hours significantly decreases antifibrinolytic efficacy.
