Clinical Advantage of Fast Aid Aerosol Technology
The Fast Aid aerosol kit eliminates direct physical contact with raw, tender, or inflamed skin. This no-touch application significantly reduces cross-contamination risks and avoids the pain associated with mechanical friction or traditional bandage placement.
